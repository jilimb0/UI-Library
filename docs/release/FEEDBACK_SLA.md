# Feedback and Support SLA

## Scope

This SLA covers issue triage, bug reports, and regression escalations for the UI library.

## Response Targets

| Priority | First Response | Mitigation Plan | Target Fix Window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 (release blocker, severe regression) | 4 business hours | 1 business day | 1-3 business days |
| P1 (major feature impairment) | 1 business day | 2 business days | 3-7 business days |
| P2 (minor bug, workaround exists) | 2 business days | 5 business days | next planned release |
| P3 (enhancement request) | 5 business days | roadmap triage | backlog |

## Channels

1. GitHub Issues for defects and feature requests.
2. Pull request comments for release-blocking regressions.

## Escalation

1. Tag issue with `priority:P0` for severe regressions.
2. Link failing CI jobs and reproduction steps.
3. Maintainers publish status updates until closure.
